Self-Introduction

An introduction to myself

Hey, welcome to my personal website!

I am Haotian Fu, a second-year PhD student majoring in Computer Engineering at Stony Brook University. Under the supervision of Prof. Fan Ye, my current research interests focus on sensing algorithms and systems for healthcare, such as indoor localization, trajectory tracking, and vital sign monitoring.

To support these research topics, I work with techniques including machine learning, deep learning, signal processing, mobile computing, and embedded systems. Currently, I have submitted a conference paper as the first author. Details are on the Publication page.

Education

I am currently pursuing my doctoral degree in Computer Engineering at ECE Department of Stony Brook University. All coursework requirement is about to be accomplished by June 2026.

Before joining Stony Brook, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan – Shanghai Jiao Tong University Joint Institute at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with minors in Computer Science, Data Science, and Global China Study.

For undergraduate research, I joined Prof. Jie Li's Intelligent Big Data System (iBDSys) Lab as a research assistant, where I mainly worked on edge computing and mobile computing. I co-authored a conference paper as the second author. More details are on the Publication page.
