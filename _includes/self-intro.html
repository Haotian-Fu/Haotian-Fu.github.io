An introduction to myself

Hey, welcome to my personal website!

I am Haotian Fu, a second-year PhD student majoring in Computer Engineering at Stony Brook University. Under the supervision of Prof. Fan Ye, my current research interests focus on sensing algorithms and systems for healthcare, such as indoor localization, trajectory tracking, and vital sign monitoring. To support these research topics, I work with techniques including machine learning, deep learning, signal processing, mobile computing, and embedded systems. Currently, I have submitted a conference paper as the first author. Details are on the Publication page.

Before joining Stony Brook, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan – Shanghai Jiao Tong University Joint Institute at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with minors in Computer Science, Data Science, and Global China Study.

During my undergraduate study, I was awarded SJTU Outstanding Graduate (上海交通大学本科优秀毕业生) and SJTU Excellent Undergraduate Scholarship (上海交通大学优秀本科生奖学金) twice. I also received other scholarships such as National Encouragement Scholarship (国家励志奖学金), UM-SJTU JI Yu Liming Scholarship (交大密西根学院本科生俞黎明奖学金), and UM-SJTU JI Academic Progress Scholarship (交大密西根学院本科生学业进步奖学金).

For undergraduate research, I joined Prof. Jie Li's Intelligent Big Data System (iBDSys) Lab as a research assistant, where I mainly worked on edge computing and mobile computing. I co-authored a conference paper as the second author. More details are on the Publication page.
