An introduction to myself

Hey, welcome to my personal website!

I am Haotian Fu, a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Stony Brook University, advised by Prof. Fan Ye (IEEE Fellow). My research interests include multimodal sensing, sensing infrastructure, and spatial intelligence, with applications in indoor localization, activity recognition, and home-health / smart-aging monitoring.

In practice, I build end-to-end sensing systems and learning pipelines spanning UWB / RF sensing, multi-sensor fusion, dataset collection and quality assessment, and deep learning on GPU clusters (PyTorch / CUDA, Slurm). I am currently seeking a 2027 summer internship in sensing systems, machine learning, or related applied research roles. See my Resume page for downloadable CVs.
